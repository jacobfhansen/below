# Map 4: The Beach

## Overview
A sandy beach where Charon the forgetfull boatman resides. There is a caved in exit that needs to be cleared.
Medusa was transported by Charon here but he forgot. Player needs The Mole to clear the rubble. But the Mole
have eaten the Herbs and is now very sick. The Mole needs The Antidote. The Hermit have the antidote but will
initially not give it to the player who have to convince him someway.

## NPC's
Charon the Boatman - A very old and forgetfull character who have absolutely no short term memory. He knows all
other NPC's in the game, but cant remember what happened five minutes ago. Speaks in dactylic hexameter. Is
rather annoyed about being reminded that he forgets stuff.

## Tone
A windswept and dark beach next to a pitch black and stone-cold lake.
