# Map 6: Deeper dark

## Overview
A dim and dark map. 50% brightness compared to other maps.

## NPC's
The shadow version of the devil. Only the silhuette is shown. No dialog. He jumps at the player forcing the player to run away.

## Puzzle
Reminds of pacman at first. Ghosts chasing player. If a ghost catches player, player is kicked out of the map back to map 5. 
Solution is to go talk with Sam Shale. Sam has a flashlight that he gives the player. Flashlight scares ghosts away.
But Sam is not activated before the player finds object "Medusa hair".
The ghosts are actually trying to help the player, preventing the player from going deeper. Preventing the player from meeting the devil.
Flashlight also have power to reveal hidden exit to map 7.
