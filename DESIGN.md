# Below — Game Design

## Story

A child falls through a hole in the ground and finds itself trapped in a strange underground world populated by eccentric characters with bizarre agendas. The purpose is to escape the maze by solving puzzles and playing the various NPC characters against each other and themselves.

There is no real combat — when the player attacks, nobody dies, but their perception of the player might change.

---

## NPCs & Relationships

### Jester
Annoying and rude. Gives misleading clues and conveys half-truths and outright lies.
- Doesn't care about the Hermit
- Fears Medusa
- Finds the Mole boring
- Loves to fool Sam Shale
- **Secret**: Appears in disguise tailing Sam Shale on Map 3. The player can reveal him, but Sam Shale ignores it — he's a character in a movie and can't hear the audience yelling "It's the Jester!" Much to the Jester's delight.

### Hermit (Alistair Wormwood)
A lost soul consumed in his own delusions. A former botanist. Trades keys for herbs (the real keys are the herbs).
- Is amused and befuddled by Medusa taking interest in his hobby
- Despises the Mole for his evil ways
- Finds the Jester annoying for teasing him all the time
- Does not get Sam Shale at all

### Medusa
Aloof and dreaming. A former "stoner" who turned people to stone with her gaze, but has reformed and no longer does that.
- Pities the Mole — herself a former evildoer
- Mostly ignores the Jester
- Finds the Hermit amusing
- Avoids Sam Shale for her own personal reasons

### The Mole
Lives in The Maze, a series of tunnels he has dug to find the herbs the Hermit has. Speaks in Shakespearean English.
- The Hermit won't give him herbs because the Mole is evil
- Has no interest in Sam Shale

### Sam Shale
Film noir detective. Endlessly searching for Medusa, lost in his fake Chicago-like cave chasing shadows.
- Believes the Jester is his contact in the underworld and numerious other characters that fit into his universe.
- Sam Slate is a two-dimensional character that only exists in his film-noir hard-bioled detective universe and
can not understand and ignores anything that fals out of that context.

## Maps & Roadmap

Up to 10 maps planned. Each map has its own theme, NPCs, and puzzles.

### Map 0 — The Hermit's Cave *(done)*
Hermit NPC, first encounters, key-for-herbs trading.

### Map 1 — The Caves *(done)*
Entrance area with statues, password door (STONER), Medusa, Jester.

### Map 2 — The Maze *(done)*
Three-section maze with Mole, traps, pushable rocks, shimmering walls, locked stone doors. Leads to hidden area with exit to Map 3.

### Map 3 — "The Depths" / Noir District *(in progress)*
- Film noir aesthetic: dark cave-city streetscape with alleys, storefronts, lamplight (lightbeam obstacles as street lamps)
- Sam Shale patrols multiple locations; each encounter advances his investigation of Medusa
- Jester disguise system: Jester icon/name/text swaps to impersonate various characters tailing Sam Shale
- Player encounters "disguised" Jester giving false noir leads
- Player can reveal the Jester but Sam Shale ignores it — he's in-character, can't hear the audience
- Real quest is finding parts for a derelict boat that is harbored on one of the piers to repair it and sail on to map 4.
- Sam Slate recognises boat parts as evidence in his investigation, but player can see what the items truly are.

### Map 4 — The Glowing Caves *(planned)*
- Bioluminescent mushrooms, glowing flora
- Lightbeam reflection puzzles: pushable mirrors/reflectors redirect lightbeams to open paths
- New NPC: mushroom farmer or glowing creature

### Map 5 — The Underground Sea *(planned)*
- Boat assembly mechanic: collect parts (planks, sail, rudder) as inventory items from maps 3–4
- Launch point + sailing across an underground sea
- New NPC: ferryman or sea creature

### Map 6 — The Library *(planned)*
- Wacky librarian NPC with confusing lore about Below's history and NPC origins
- Books with clues about NPC relationships, weaknesses, passwords
- Cipher puzzles, catalog system
- **Victory**: after Map 6, exit to the surface

### Maps 7–10 *(open)*
Reserved for future inspiration.

## Cross-map Systems

- **NPC opinion web**: dialog options unlocked based on other NPCs' states and flags
- **Jester disguise system**: reusable across maps — Jester appears in different forms to different NPCs
- **Inventory items used for assembly/puzzles**: boat parts, keys, herbs, clues from one map solve puzzles on another
